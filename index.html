Services

Everything your yard needs, under one crew.

Whether it's a bare new-build block or a tired backyard, we plan the whole space and build it in the right order, so nothing gets dug up twice.

Paving & paths

Patios, driveways, stepping paths and pool surrounds, laid on a properly compacted base so they stay flat.

Retaining walls

Stone, block and sleeper walls that tame a sloping block and give you level, usable ground.

Turf & lawns

Soil preparation, levelling and instant turf, plus synthetic grass where you want green without the mowing.

Garden beds & planting

Plant selection that suits Melbourne's weather, mulching, edging and garden beds that fill out well.

Outdoor living areas

Entertaining areas, fire pit zones and seating nooks designed around how you actually use your yard.

Garden clean-ups

Overgrown yard, pre-sale tidy or a seasonal reset. Mowing, hedging, pruning and green waste removed.

How it works

Four steps from first call to finished garden.

  1. 01

    Call or text

    Tell us what you have in mind. We'll book a time to come out and see the site.

  2. 02

    Site visit & quote

    We measure up, talk through options and materials, and give you a clear written quote.

  3. 03

    Design & plan

    Layout, levels, materials and plants are agreed before any work starts. No surprises.

  4. 04

    Build & hand over

    We build it, clean up after ourselves, and walk you through how to look after it.
